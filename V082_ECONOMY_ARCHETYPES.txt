V0.8.2 ECONOMY + VENTURE ARCHETYPES

Venture categories now behave differently:
- F&B: high demand, operating-cost sensitivity
- Automotive: high ticket, high margin, higher volatility
- Professional Service: stable recurring model
- Construction: highest project upside, payment/project risk
- Gaming & Retail: balanced ecosystem model
- Manufacturing: strong scaling, higher capital risk
- Education: stable, reputation-driven
- Event & Venue: seasonal high-value booking
- Retail and Service fallback models

FUNCTIONAL CHANGES
- Archetype multiplier affects projected venture income.
- Archetype scale multiplier affects venture upgrades.
- Venture risk falls slightly as level increases.
- Marketing Executive gives projected revenue bonus.
- CEO Marketing gives a larger temporary projected boost.
- Diversified portfolios get 2% / 5% / 8% projected-income bonus at 2 / 3 / 4+ archetypes.
- Added archetype-themed economic events.
- Economy panels show projected income, level, growth and risk where venture details support it.
