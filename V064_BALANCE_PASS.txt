KELANTAN NEXT CEO : START UP — V0.6.4 BALANCE PASS

Core balance changes:
- Starting cash remains equal for all Founders.
- Staff now directly affects business revenue:
  0 staff = founder-operated at 45% base revenue.
  Full staff = 100% revenue.
- Manager gives +10% efficiency and frees Owner Capacity.
- Owner Capacity remains 2 unmanaged businesses.
- Hire cost scales with business size: RM1k / RM1.5k / RM2.5k.
- Manager cost scales: RM5k / RM10k.
- SME Loan requires Reputation 2.
- Growth Loan requires Reputation 5.
- Bankrupt Founder is removed from future turns when debt exceeds total assets and cash is zero.
- Cashflow skips bankrupt players and automatically advances to the next active Founder.
- Player card now shows Net Worth.
- Portfolio shows estimated business cashflow.
- Rules book updated with business balance and bankruptcy rules.

Design goal:
Luck can create opportunities/problems, but staffing, leverage, cash reserves, managers and negotiation determine who scales.
