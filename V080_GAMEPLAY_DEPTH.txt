KELANTAN NEXT CEO : START UP — V0.8 GAMEPLAY DEPTH

NEW: CEO ACTION
One strategic action per round:
- INVEST: upgrade an owned venture
- NETWORK: stores an opportunity boost
- RECRUIT: opens Talent Market
- MARKETING: RM2,000 activation and stores marketing boost
- SAVE CASH: stores a defensive cash shield

NEW: VENTURE LEVELS
Level 1 Startup → Level 2 Growing → Level 3 Established → Level 4 Expansion → Level 5 Market Leader.
Upgrade cost scales with level. Revenue/value increase when upgraded.

NEW: TALENT MARKET
- Marketing Executive
- Operations Manager (owner capacity +1)
- Accountant
- Sales Closer
Talent is stored on the player and recruitment costs cash.

FOUNDATION: EVENT DECK CATEGORIES
Market / Opportunity / Crisis / Network / Kelantan Event labels are defined for the next event-reveal integration.

Existing Firebase multiplayer, dice, pawn movement, venture ownership, audio and responsive work are preserved.
