V0.7.2 RESPONSIVE RECOVERY

The forced-landscape experiment from V0.7.1 is removed.

PORTRAIT PHONE
- Board remains square and nearly full phone width.
- Compact HUD above the board.
- Bottom navigation remains at bottom.
- No rotate blocker and no forced screen.orientation lock.

LANDSCAPE PHONE
- Board scales primarily from viewport height, not width.
- Compact HUD stays above board.
- Navigation becomes a narrow right rail.
- Safe right padding prevents navigation from covering the board.

DESKTOP/TABLET
- Removes the overly aggressive generic two-column .app/.game-shell rule.
- Content returns to a stable centered layout.
- Board is no longer forced sticky.

Goal: orientation should adapt to the player, not fight the player.
