V0.7.4 CROSS-PLATFORM PAWN STEP FEEDBACK

ANDROID / SUPPORTED BROWSERS
- Keeps real 10ms vibration on every pawn step.
- Adds a subtle step click and pawn pulse.

IPHONE / IOS PWA
- Web Vibration API is unavailable, so every pawn step now has:
  1. short audible click,
  2. micro-hop animation,
  3. brief gold pulse/glow.
- This gives a clear tactile-like rhythm without pretending iOS web vibration exists.

Sound obeys the existing SFX volume/toggle.
