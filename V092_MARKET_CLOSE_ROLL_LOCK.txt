V0.9.2 VENTURE MARKET CLOSE + HARD ROLL LOCK

VENTURE MARKET
- The X button now has its own explicit ID and click handler.
- Tap outside the Venture Market modal to close.
- Escape closes it on desktop.
- Pointer/touch handling reinforced.

ROLL
- Added three simultaneous guards:
  1. room.rollConsumedUid
  2. room.lastRolledTurnSerial
  3. player.lastRollTurnSerial
- All three are checked atomically in Firestore before a roll is accepted.
- Rapid double tapping cannot create a second roll.
- Roll lock is cleared ONLY when END TURN passes control to the next Founder.
- MARKET can no longer be pressed in the middle of an active turn to reset the roll state.
