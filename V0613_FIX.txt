KELANTAN NEXT CEO : START UP — V0.6.13

FIX 1 — DICE ANIMATION
The V0.6.12 audio engine had a recursion bug:
ensureAudio() -> startMusicLoop() -> ensureAudio().
When dice SFX started, that loop could stop execution before animateDoubleDice() ran.
V0.6.13 removes that recursion and makes audio non-blocking, so the cinematic two-dice animation remains the gameplay priority.

FIX 2 — MAP DISTRICT CIRCLES
Removed the circular .node markers that sat above Kelantan district names.
The Kelantan map now shows the district names/artwork cleanly without those dots.
They were legacy movement nodes from the old map-based movement system and are no longer needed because pawn movement now uses the outer board track.
