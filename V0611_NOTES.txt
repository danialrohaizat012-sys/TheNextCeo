UNIQUE VENTURES
- A real venture can only have ONE owner at a time.
- Marketplace marks purchased ventures as OWNED + Founder name.
- Purchased venture cannot be acquired again from the market.
- Acquisition uses a Firestore transaction and room.ventureOwners registry to prevent two players buying the same venture at nearly the same time.
- Nego Deal remains valid: if a venture is sold to another Founder, ownership registry transfers to the buyer.
