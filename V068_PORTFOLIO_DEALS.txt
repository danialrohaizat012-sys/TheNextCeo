V0.6.8 PORTFOLIO NEGOTIATION
- Buyer selects another Founder.
- Buyer sees that Founder's current business portfolio.
- Buyer selects one business and enters an offer price.
- Seller receives a live incoming-offer popup.
- Seller can ACCEPT or REJECT.
- On ACCEPT, Firestore transaction verifies buyer cash and seller ownership, transfers cash to seller, and transfers the exact business to buyer.
- On REJECT, no cash or business moves.
- If buyer spends the money or seller no longer owns the business before acceptance, the deal fails safely.
