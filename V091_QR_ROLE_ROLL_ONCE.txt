V0.9.1 CORRECT QR FLOW + ONE ROLL PER TURN

QR DEAL FLOW
1. Buyer makes offer.
2. Seller accepts terms.
3. Seller phone automatically shows acquisition QR.
4. Buyer gets Complete Acquisition popup.
5. Buyer taps SCAN SELLER QR.
6. Buyer camera scanner opens.
7. Buyer scans seller QR, reviews amount, then CONFIRM & PAY.
8. Cash and venture transfer atomically.
9. PAY ONLINE remains available for remote play.

Deal QR now encodes room + deal ID directly and validates against room.deals.
It no longer needs a separate payment document for acquisition QR.

TURN SYSTEM
- Added room.turnSerial + room.lastRolledTurnSerial.
- Roll is guarded by a Firestore transaction, so rapid double taps cannot roll twice.
- Exactly ONE roll is allowed for each player turn.
- After rolling and landing, phase becomes ACTION.
- New END TURN button passes control to the next active Founder and unlocks one fresh roll.
- Roll button shows ROLL USED after the turn's roll is consumed.
